## The look: "Paper Ops Room"

| | |
|---|---|
| **World** | A cut-paper office world on a **cool white paper** ground with a fine fibre texture. Every piece is a flat cut shape with an uneven hand-cut edge and a **deep, soft drop shadow**, so layers lift off the page. Grown-up, not cartoon: restrained faces, no bouncing, cinematic vignette and light falloff. |
| **Palette** | Blue `#4285F4` (You, people, trust) · Red `#EA4335` (the AI, risk, "erased") · Yellow `#FBBC05` (numbers, highlights, chapter tags) · Green `#34A853` (the new role, success) · Orange `#F5820D` (the Manager, warnings, money) · White `#FFFFFF` (paper, UI). Plus one **deep ink navy** `#14213D` for text, shadows and the dark chapter cards: that contrast is what makes the colours pop and keeps it adult. |
| **Type** | Chapter numbers in **Archivo Black** on yellow paper tags. Key phrases in **Bricolage Grotesque 800** (one per beat, 1–3 words, 120 px+ where it's the hero). Handwritten notes in **Caveat**. UI text in **Inter**. |
| **Characters** | **You**: the hero, blue cardigan, plays every job (a different prop or badge per chapter: visor, headset, calculator, dictionary…). **The Agent**: the AI, a red paper robot with a lanyard, wide eyes, eager and literal. It appears whenever the voice describes what AI does. **The Manager**: orange blazer. **Cast**: the café owner, angry VIP client, experts, interviewers, couples at the bank, co-workers. |
| **Acting** | Paper-puppet acting: limbs hinge, heads tilt, blinks and breathing are automatic, mouth swaps land on the word (gulp on "next", smile on "switch"). |
| **Camera** | One virtual camera over a layered paper set (back, middle, front move at different speeds). One move per beat, 1.0–1.6 s, eased in and out; between moves a ~1 % drift so nothing freezes. **Isometric 3D tilt** is the signature move: every chapter opens on a 3D paper diorama of that job's desk, then the camera tilts down into the flat acting scene. Also used for the "300 workers" crowd, the database, the device wall, the 10,000-page stack and the final "run update". No shakes except two motivated impacts (0:05 and 10:30). |
| **UI** | Clean flat screens inside paper laptops and phones: a generic **AI chat** (prompt typed in sync with your voice, fill-in chips highlighted), a social **feed**, a shared **spreadsheet**, a **database audit** table, a **bank feed**, a **profile page**, a **design tool**, a **calendar**, a **comment box**. Product names are spoken, **no real logos**. |
| **Learning layer** | **THE SWITCH BOARD**: a departure-board panel with 10 rows. Each row starts red (the job). At each "You should switch to…" the row **flips green** to the new role. It appears in the intro, in a corner at every switch, and complete at the end, so viewers always know where they are and get a one-screen summary to screenshot. Every prompt is labelled **PROMPT 01–10** with a "pause & copy" sticky note. |
| **Chapter rhythm** | Every job has the same four acts so it's easy to follow: **① Card** (iso desk + "0X/10" + AT RISK) → **② The threat** (what AI now does, shown word by word) → **③ The switch** (board row flips, costume change, new-role banner) → **④ The prompt** (typed on screen). Inside that rhythm each chapter gets its own set, colours and gag so it never feels repeated. |

## Sound (all generated in Python, no piano)

- **Music:** an original score, mixed **about 25 dB under the voice** (your request: −20 dB or lower), changing with each scene:
  **Pulse** (dark synth-pluck pulse, clock ticks: the hook and every "threat" act) → **Groove** (marimba, bass, soft kick, shaker: the switch and the prompt) → **Night** (sparse kalimba and pads: Job 2's 2 a.m.) → **Data** (tight pizzicato ostinato: numbers and spreadsheets) → **Lift** (bright, kalimba sparkle: outro). Key changes per chapter so 10 chapters don't loop the same bed; short drops to near-silence on the big lines ("your job is next", "AI is not erasing work itself").
- **SFX:** paper slides and flips, card flicks for the board rows (a soft split-flap clatter on each flip), keyboard ticks under every typed prompt, scanner sweep, stamp thunk ("AT RISK"), cash register (soft), phone buzz, chat knock, stopwatch tick + ding, whoosh for camera tilts, eraser rub, crowd murmur, notification ping. Everything sits under the voice.
