# HOWL — screenplay (draft 1)

**Runtime:** about 9 minutes · **Format:** 16:9 for YouTube (vertical cut-downs for Shorts later)
**Look:** cinematic 3D / 2.5D city (dark, foggy, moonlit, sodium street lights, broken cars, ivy on brick) with the
stylised cartoon characters from the reference picture: black faces, big white eyes, simple bold clothes. Zombies:
green heads, glowing yellow eyes, torn dark clothes, arms out.
**Inspired by** the fast, swarming, sound-hunting outbreak of *World War Z*, but an original story and characters.

---

## The cast (matches the reference picture)

| Name | Look | Who they are | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SAMI** (lead) | Red T-shirt, jeans, messy brown hair | Track runner. Fast, decisive, hides fear behind jokes. Leads the group. | Young male, 17-18, warm, breathless when running |
| **MAYA** | Yellow hoodie, brown ponytail | Careful one. Notices everything. **She narrates the film, years later.** | Young female (dialogue) / same voice older, calm, low (narration) |
| **ZOE** | Blonde bob, denim jacket, red skirt | Bold, sarcastic, brave. Hides a secret in Act 3. | Young female, quick, sharp |
| **THEO** | Curly dark hair, dark T-shirt | Loud, funny, always filming on his phone. | Young male, high energy, cracks under pressure |
| **RICO** | Backwards cap, green flannel | Big heart, protective. **It is his 18th birthday.** | Young male, deep, easy-going |
| **The Horde** | Green zombies | Blind-ish. They hunt by **sound** and **howl** to call each other. | Layered howls, shrieks, clicks |

**The rules of this world** (taught one at a time, each one costs the group something):
1. A bite turns you. A bite to the head or neck: about **ten seconds**.
2. They don't see you. **They hear you.**
3. When one howls, they all come.
4. A bite on an arm or leg is **slower**. Hours, sometimes.

---

## Retention map (why people keep watching)

| Time | Hook / re-hook |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Black screen, one howl, a thousand answer. Then straight into the chase. |
| 0:20 | The promise: *"Five of us ran. Two reached the water. Only one of us is still telling this story."* Who? |
| 1:40 | First turning on screen, counted aloud to ten. |
| 2:30 | The phone rings in the alley. Rule 2 learned the hard way. |
| 3:40 | A face pressed against the laundromat glass, listening. |
| 4:30 | Car alarm. The horde floods the bridge. Rico is bitten. |
| 5:40 | Rico's sacrifice. Silence + music only. |
| 6:30 | The wall at the harbour. The ferry horn. The zombie pile climbs the wall. |
| 7:20 | Theo is lost. Sami counts to ten. |
| 8:30 | The twist: Zoe's hidden bite. Cliffhanger for Part 2. |

Rhythm rule for the edit: never more than about 60 seconds without a new threat, a new rule or a loss. And three
planned **no-dialogue moments** (marked `[NO WORDS]`) where only music and sound carry the scene.

---

## How to read this script

- `MAYA (V.O.)` = narration (older Maya, looking back).
- `NAME:` = dialogue spoken in the scene.
- `[tags]` inside lines are performance directions written for ElevenLabs (e.g. `[whispering]`, `[panting]`).
- **SFX** = sound effects · **MUSIC** = score · **SHOT** = camera.
- Times are a plan. The recorded voice sets the final timing.

# COLD OPEN

### 1. BLACK — 0:00–0:04
**SFX:** Total silence. One long, wet, distant **howl**. Then, from every direction, hundreds answer it.
**MUSIC:** A low sub-bass hit on the last howl.

### 2. EXT. DEAD STREET — NIGHT — 0:04–0:14  *(the reference picture)*
**SHOT:** Low, fast push-in on the five friends sprinting straight at camera. Behind them the horde floods the street,
climbing over wrecked cars. Fog, moon, flickering street lights. "RUN!" sprayed on the wall.
**SFX:** Feet slapping wet asphalt, panting, a car alarm dying, howls getting closer.

> SAMI: [shouting, panting] Don't look back! Whatever you hear — **don't look back!**

### 3. EXT. DEAD STREET — CONTINUOUS — 0:14–0:20
**SHOT:** Over-the-shoulder from behind the horde: zombies pour over a car roof like water over a dam. A zombie on top
of a car throws its head back and **howls**. Every head in the street snaps toward the sound.

### 4. EXT. ROOFTOP — DAWN (FLASH-FORWARD) — 0:20–0:32
**SHOT:** Slow pull-out. A yellow hoodie hangs on a ferry railing in grey morning light. The burning city behind.
**MUSIC:** Single piano note, held.

> MAYA (V.O.): [calm, low, tired] There were five of us that night.
> Two of us reached the water.
> [pause] And only one of us... is still telling this story.

### 5. TITLE — 0:32–0:38
**SHOT:** Black. The word **HOWL** cuts in, scratched and green-lit. A final howl rips through it.
**MUSIC:** Hit, then silence.

# ACT ONE — THE LAST NORMAL NIGHT

### 6. EXT. ROOFTOP — DUSK — 0:38–1:05
**Caption:** *Six hours earlier.*
**SHOT:** Wide golden-hour skyline. Fairy lights strung between chimneys. A lopsided cake with a candle shaped like an
"18". Theo films everything on his phone.
**MUSIC:** Warm, light, a bit nostalgic.
**SFX:** City hum, distant traffic, wind.

> THEO: [hyped, filming] Ladies and gentlemen, Rico is officially an adult. Say something wise, old man.
> RICO: [laughing] My knees hurt.
> ZOE: [deadpan] Wise.
> MAYA: Make a wish, Rico.
> RICO: [blows the candle] Already did.
> SAMI: What was it?
> RICO: [grinning] That we do this again next year. All five of us.

### 7. EXT. ROOFTOP — DUSK — 1:05–1:20
**SHOT:** Slow tilt up. Helicopters cross the orange sky in a line, far too many of them. Nobody looks up.

> MAYA (V.O.): The sky was full of helicopters that night.
> [beat] We were too busy laughing to look up.

### 8. EXT. ROOFTOP — PHONE CLOSE-UP — 1:20–1:35
**SHOT:** Theo's phone, a news livestream.
**SFX:** Tinny phone audio.

> NEWS ANCHOR (on phone): [tense, professional] ...unconfirmed reports of violent attacks in several cities. Officials
> are urging residents to stay indoors and to stay... quiet.
> THEO: [swipes it away] Bro, it's the end of the world on the news every week.
> ZOE: Stay *quiet*? Who says that?

### 9. EXT. ROOFTOP — NIGHT — 1:35–1:45  `[NO WORDS]`
**SHOT:** Wide. The city's lights go out district by district, a wave of black rolling toward them. Their rooftop goes
dark last.
**SFX:** Each district dies with a deep electrical *thunk*. Then nothing. Total silence.
Then, far away: one **howl**. Another answers. Closer. A chain of howls, racing toward them.

### 10. EXT. STREET BELOW — NIGHT — 1:45–2:10
**SHOT:** Looking down over the roof edge. Under one surviving street light a man staggers, holding his neck. A woman
helps him sit down. He shakes.

> MAYA: [whispering, almost without knowing] One... two... three...
> SAMI: Maya, what are you doing?
> MAYA: [whispering] ...seven... eight... nine...

**SHOT:** Push in hard on the man. His head snaps up. Glowing yellow eyes.

> MAYA: [barely audible] ...ten.

**SFX:** He **howls**. The woman screams. From every alley the howl is answered.

> MAYA (V.O.): Ten seconds. That's how long it took to stop being a person.

### 11. EXT. ROOFTOP — CONTINUOUS — 2:10–2:20

> ZOE: [shaky] Tell me that's a prank. Theo, tell me that's one of your pranks.
> SAMI: [hard, fast] Stairs. Now. **Go!**

---

# ACT TWO — THE RULES

### 12. INT. STAIRWELL — NIGHT — 2:20–2:35
**SHOT:** Red emergency light. The five race down. Below them, a door bursts open: pounding feet coming **up**.
**SFX:** Echoing footsteps, metal railings, a door slamming, howls bouncing up the stairwell.

> SAMI: Not down! Fire escape — through here!

### 13. EXT. ALLEY — NIGHT — 2:35–3:05
**SHOT:** They crouch behind a dumpster. At the mouth of the alley, zombies pass slowly, heads tilting, sniffing,
listening. One stops. Turns its head toward the dumpster. Holds.
**MUSIC:** Drops out. Only breathing.

> SAMI: [whispering] Nobody. Move.

**SHOT:** Close on Theo's pocket. A glow.
**SFX:** Theo's ringtone — loud, cheerful, horribly wrong. Screen reads *MOM*.
**SHOT:** Every zombie head snaps toward them at once.

> THEO: [panicked whisper] No no no no—
> SAMI: **RUN!**

> MAYA (V.O.): That's how we learned the first rule.
> They don't see you. [beat] They **hear** you.

### 14. EXT. DEAD STREET — NIGHT — 3:05–3:20
**SHOT:** The chase from the cold open, now in context. Sami leads them toward a police barricade and a jammed bus.
They squeeze through the gap under the bus one by one, Rico last, pulling Maya by the hood.
**SFX:** Howls, hands slamming metal behind them, the bus rocking.

### 15. INT. LAUNDROMAT — NIGHT — 3:20–3:50
**SHOT:** Blue light from the machines. The five sit on the floor, backs to the dryers, chests heaving.
`[NO WORDS — 6 seconds]`: only breathing, a dripping tap, howls far away.

> THEO: [voice cracking] That was my fault. That was my mom. I didn't— I couldn't—
> RICO: [quiet, kind] Hey. Hey. You turned it off. We're here. All five.
> ZOE: [shaky laugh] Happy birthday, by the way.
> RICO: Best one yet.

**SHOT:** Maya finds a battery radio on a shelf. Static, then a voice.

> RADIO VOICE: [crackling, official] ...evacuation ferries leave **Pier Nine** until **sunrise**. Gates close at
> sunrise. A bite to the head or neck turns in seconds. A bite to the arm or leg may take longer. Do **not** bring the
> infected. Repeat: do not bring the inf— [static]

> MAYA (V.O.): Pier Nine was three miles away. Sunrise was five hours away.
> SAMI: Three miles. I run that every morning.
> ZOE: Not with *them* behind you.
> SAMI: [small smile] Then I'll run faster.

### 16. INT. LAUNDROMAT — CONTINUOUS — 3:50–4:05
**SHOT:** Slow push toward the front window. A zombie face is pressed against the glass. Cloudy eyes. It isn't looking.
It's **listening**. Its breath fogs the glass.
**SFX:** A wet click from its throat.

> SAMI: [silently mouths: back door]

They crawl away on hands and knees. Nobody breathes.

---

# ACT TWO B — THE PRICE

### 17. EXT. BRIDGE RAMP — NIGHT — 4:05–4:30
**SHOT:** A highway ramp jammed with abandoned cars, doors open, headlights still on. Mist. The harbour lights glow far
away on the other side.

> SAMI: [whispering] Don't touch the cars. Any of them.

**SHOT:** Zoe steps over a bumper. Her foot slips. Her hand slams onto a car bonnet.
**SFX:** A car alarm. Then another wakes. Then ten more. The whole ramp screams.

> ZOE: [horrified] No—

### 18. EXT. BRIDGE RAMP — CONTINUOUS — 4:30–4:55
**SHOT:** High wide: from every side street, the horde floods onto the ramp like rivers joining.
**SHOT:** The five sprint between the cars. Rico boosts Maya onto the roof of a city bus. As he climbs up after her, a
hand grabs his arm. Teeth. He screams, kicks free, and rolls onto the roof.
**SFX:** The bite. Rico's scream. Maya's scream. Fists hammering the bus walls below.

### 19. EXT. BUS ROOF — CONTINUOUS — 4:55–5:30
**SHOT:** Close on Rico's forearm. A bite. The green already creeping under the skin. He pulls his flannel sleeve
down over it.
**MUSIC:** The alarms and howls fade under a low, sad drone.

> MAYA: [crying] Your arm. The radio said arms take longer. We get you to the pier, there's doctors, there's—
> RICO: [gently] Maya. You heard the radio. They won't let me on.
> SAMI: Then we find another way.
> RICO: [looking down] Listen to them, Sami. They go where the noise is.

**SHOT:** Rico looks across the ramp: a fire truck, doors open, keys hanging.

> RICO: [a small, brave laugh] So let's give them a party.

### 20. EXT. BRIDGE RAMP — NIGHT — 5:30–6:05
**SHOT:** Rico drops from the bus and runs to the fire truck. He hits the siren, the horn, every light.
**SFX:** The siren howls louder than the horde.
**SHOT:** Every zombie turns away from the bus, toward the truck.

> RICO: [yelling out the window, voice breaking into a laugh] Hey! It's my birthday! **Everybody's invited!**

**SHOT:** The truck pulls away down the ramp, red lights spinning, the whole horde following it like a river.
From the bus roof, four silhouettes watch.

`[NO WORDS — 15 seconds]`
**MUSIC:** A slow, broken piano theme (Rico's theme, from the rooftop scene, now played slowly).
**SFX:** The siren gets smaller... smaller... then stops. Silence. One distant howl.

> MAYA (V.O.): [very quiet] He got his wish.
> We did do it again the next year.
> [beat] Just not all five of us.

---

# ACT THREE — THE WATER

### 21. EXT. HARBOUR WALL — PRE-DAWN — 6:05–6:30
**SHOT:** Wide. A huge concrete wall with floodlights, razor wire, soldiers on top. Behind it, the ferry, lit up,
smoke from its funnel. Hundreds of survivors pressed at one gate. The sky starting to turn blue.
**SFX:** Crowd murmur, soldiers shouting, a loudspeaker.

> LOUDSPEAKER: [echoing] Gates close at sunrise. Remain calm. Remain **quiet**.

**SHOT:** Zoe walks apart from the others, holding her right wrist, jacket sleeve pulled low over her hand.

> MAYA: You okay?
> ZOE: [too quickly] Hit it on that car. It's fine.

*(Plant: hold on her sleeve for one beat too long.)*

### 22. EXT. HARBOUR WALL — DAWN — 6:30–6:50
**SHOT:** The first ray of sun touches the ferry. On deck, a sailor pulls the cord.
**SFX:** The ferry **HORN**. Huge. It echoes across the whole city.
**SHOT:** Silence for one beat. Every survivor's face freezes.

> SAMI: [whispering] ...oh no.

**SFX:** From the city, ten thousand howls answer the horn at once.

### 23. EXT. HARBOUR WALL — CONTINUOUS — 6:50–7:15
**SHOT:** The horde hits the outside of the wall and **climbs itself**: zombies pile on zombies, a living ramp rising
up the concrete toward the soldiers. Floodlights swing. Gunfire flashes.
**SHOT:** The gate starts to grind shut. The crowd surges.

> SOLDIER: [shouting] Gate's closing! Get through! **Get through!**

### 24. EXT. HARBOUR GATE — CONTINUOUS — 7:15–7:40
**SHOT:** The five — four now — fight through the crowd. Theo shoves Maya and Zoe through the narrowing gap. Hands
grab Theo from behind. He looks back at the wave coming.
He presses his phone into Maya's hand through the bars.

> THEO: [fast, desperate, smiling through tears] It's all on there. Everything. Make them see it, Maya. **Make them see.**

**SHOT:** The crowd pulls him back. He's gone.

### 25. EXT. HARBOUR GATE — CONTINUOUS — 7:40–8:20
**SHOT:** The gate jams half-open, motor smoking. The horde is coming. Sami is still outside. He grabs the manual
crank and heaves. The gate shudders shut, Maya on the inside, Sami on the outside.
**SHOT:** A zombie lunges from the crowd and bites Sami's shoulder. He shoves it off. Looks at the wound. Looks at Maya.
**MUSIC:** Cuts out. Only the gate rattling and far-off howls.

> MAYA: [through the bars] Sami — Sami, no—
> SAMI: [breathing hard, calm] Hey. Count for me.
> MAYA: [sobbing] No.
> SAMI: [softly] Count. Slow. Like on the roof.
> MAYA: [whispering] ...one... two... three...
> SAMI: [smiles] You always count too fast.
> MAYA: ...four... five...

**SHOT:** Sami lets go of the bars. Turns. He looks at the horde, then at the sunrise behind it. And he **runs**:
straight along the wall, shouting, drawing the whole horde away from the gate.

> SAMI: [shouting, running away] **HEY! OVER HERE!**
> MAYA: [whispering] ...nine...

**SHOT:** A tiny red shirt disappearing into the sunlight.

> MAYA: [barely] ...ten.

**SFX:** Cut to **total silence.** Black screen, 2 seconds.

### 26. EXT. FERRY — MORNING — 8:20–8:40  `[NO WORDS]`
**SHOT:** High, slow drone pull-back. The ferry leaves the harbour. The city burns in gold morning light. Smoke,
gulls, the wall swarming with tiny green figures.
**MUSIC:** The full theme, finally, big and sad.
**SFX:** Waves, engine, the howls getting fainter.

> MAYA (V.O.): [quiet] Five of us ran that night.
> Two of us reached the water.

### 27. INT. FERRY DECK — MORNING — 8:40–9:00
**SHOT:** Zoe sits alone against the railing, sleeve pulled over her wrist. Maya sits down next to her and takes her
hand. Zoe flinches. Maya turns the hand over and slides the sleeve back.
A bite. Green veins crawling toward the elbow.
**MUSIC:** One held, high note.

> ZOE: [whispering, scared] It's been six hours. It's slow, on the wrist. [beat] Don't tell them. Please.

**SHOT:** Maya looks at the soldiers at the end of the deck. Back at Zoe. Closes the sleeve. Holds her hand.

> MAYA (V.O.): I didn't tell them.

**SHOT:** Extreme close-up: Zoe's eyes. A flicker of yellow in them.
**SFX:** From below deck, very faint... one **howl**.

### 28. END CARD — 9:00
**SHOT:** Black. **HOWL** — then, under it: **PART 2**.
**SFX:** Theo's ringtone, once, cheerful, from his phone in Maya's pocket. Cut.

## Sound design palette

| Sound | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single howl → a thousand answer | Cold open, scene 9, scene 22 | The film's signature. Same sound every time so the audience learns to fear it. |
| Theo's ringtone | Scene 13, end card | Cheerful = horrifying. Bookends the film. |
| Car alarms waking in a chain | Scene 17 | Build from one to a wall of noise in 3 seconds. |
| Fire truck siren fading to silence | Scene 20 | Hold the silence after it stops for 2 full seconds. |
| Ferry horn | Scene 22 | The loudest sound in the film. |
| Breathing, heartbeats, dripping water | Alley, laundromat | Every hiding scene drops the music completely. |
| City ambience | Throughout | Wind, distant sirens, helicopters, burning crackle. |

**Music:** Rico's warm theme on the rooftop → the same theme on slow, broken piano after his sacrifice → full and
orchestral on the ferry. Hiding scenes: no music, only drones and breathing. Chase scenes: low drums and hits.

**Rule for the mix:** voices always on top. Music and effects duck under every line.
