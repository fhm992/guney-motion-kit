# How to Protect Your Job from AI: storyboard

**Length:** 8:41 (voiceover 521.6 s) · **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 60 fps · **No captions**
**Audience:** professionals 30+ on LinkedIn and YouTube · **Tone:** calm, smart, a little wry. The story treats the fear seriously and stays hopeful.

---

## The look: "Paper Office"

| | |
|---|---|
| **World** | One office built out of cut paper, set out like a diorama on a cream paper ground with a fine fibre texture. Every element is a flat cut shape with a soft drop shadow underneath so it seems to sit on its own layer. Edges are slightly uneven, like they were cut by hand. |
| **Palette** | **Cream paper** `#EFE6D3` (ground) · **Ink navy** `#1E2A44` (people, furniture, text) · **Tomato** `#E4572E` (AI, danger, "easy bricks") · **Sage** `#7FA88B` (human value, "safe") · Kraft brown `#C9A57A` and off-white `#FBF7EE` as neutrals. Skin tones are flat paper colours. |
| **Type** | Chapter numbers in **Archivo Black**, cut out of paper. Key phrases in **Bricolage Grotesque 800**, 120 px or larger. Handwritten notes and sticky notes in **Caveat**. |
| **Characters** | **You**: the hero, mid-30s, navy cardigan, a mug that never leaves your hand. **The Manager** ("her"): sharp bob haircut, blazer, laptop under her arm. **The Intern**: the AI, drawn as a small tomato-red paper robot with wide eyes, a lanyard and too much coffee. It appears every time the voice describes how AI behaves. **Cast extras**: the angry client (on a Zoom tile), co-workers, the new hire, Sales and Support. |
| **Acting** | Paper-puppet acting: limbs hinge at paper joints, heads tilt, eyes blink, and a mouth swap marks a reaction (gulp, smile, frown). Characters "breathe" with a 2 % scale cycle. Each emotion lands on its word: "stomach in a knot" gives a gulp and a tightening knot icon. |
| **Camera** | One virtual camera moves over a wide paper set. Moves are slow, eased dolly-ins, pans and pull-outs (0.8 to 1.4 s, ease-in-out). Foreground, middle and back layers move at different speeds so the paper set has depth. **At most one move per beat.** Between moves the camera drifts at about 1 % so no frame freezes. No shakes or whip-pans except two motivated "impact" moments (0:06 and 6:22). |
| **UI** | Screens are paper-cut laptops and phones. The interfaces inside them are clean and flat so they read clearly: a generic AI chat window (prompt typed in, answer arriving line by line), a team chat ping, a spreadsheet, a form, a Zoom grid, an email inbox. Product names are spoken in the VO but **no real logos**: the chat window is a neutral "AI chat". |
| **Learning layer** | **The Job Shield.** A paper shield builds up across the video, one cut piece per method. Each chapter opens with a paper-tag card (`01 / 09`) and closes by snapping its piece onto the shield, so viewers always know where they are. The full shield appears in the outro. |
| **On-screen text** | Not captions. Only the chapter title and one key phrase per beat (1 to 3 words), used where it helps the lesson. Every prompt the VO reads is typed into the chat UI so viewers can pause and copy it. |

## Sound (all generated in Python)

- **Music:** an original warm "paper-jazz" bed: felt piano, brushed snare, upright-style bass and a soft pad at about 92 BPM, in D major with a tense minor colour for the hook. Three variations: **Tension** (hook), **Groove** (chapters 1 to 9) and **Lift** (outro). The music ducks about 9 dB under the voice and swells a little on each chapter card.
- **SFX palette:** paper slide, paper flip, scissor snip, stamp thunk, pen scribble, sticky-note slap, soft keyboard ticks, chat "knock" ping, stopwatch tick plus ding, cash-register "ka-ching" (soft), crumple, whoosh (paper), and a low "dread" swell. Everything sits under the voice.
